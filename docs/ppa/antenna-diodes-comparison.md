# Antenna trial: T44 baseline vs heuristic diodes at 45 % utilization

Hand-written comparison of the antenna trial against the T44 baseline
(`docs/ppa/t44-ppa-table.md`). It is **not** generated by `ppa_table.py` and
does not replace the T44 table: no gate, Pareto front or D7 claim of T44 rests
on it.

- **Time rows: committed evidence.** The six time runs were collected with
  `ppa_stage.py collect --antenna-trial` (gate-level vector matching and
  annotated power, as in T41) into `sim/python/artifacts/antenna-trial/time/`;
  every number of the time tables below comes from its `rows.json` and
  `evidence_manifest.json`.
- **Frequency rows: unverified.** The F-serial trial runs are local
  (`.build/ppa/`) and not collected; their numbers cannot be checked from the
  repo.

## What changed

`ppa_stage.py run --antenna-trial` adds `antenna_trial.overrides` from
`openlane/ppa_runs.json` to the floorplan pre-pass and to the full run:

| Variable | T44 baseline | Trial |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `RUN_HEURISTIC_DIODE_INSERTION` | false | **true** |
| `GRT_ANTENNA_ITERS` | 3 | **10** |
| `GRT_ANTENNA_MARGIN` | 10 | **20** |
| `FP_CORE_UTIL` | 55 | **45** |

Everything else is the T44 setup: OpenLane 2.3.10 Classic, sky130A /
sky130_fd_sc_hd, signoff at `tt_025C_1v80`, same SDCs, same die floor rule.

## Effect on the T44 conclusions

- **Time domain, antenna.** All six time runs end with 0 antenna nets, so the
  amendment is not needed for them under the trial.
- **Price.** +14 % to +18 % cell area and +5 % to +8 % power and energy per
  output on every time run (diodes, larger die, more tap/decap cells).
- **Time domain, verdicts.** Unchanged. T-S8P1-FAST keeps passing (+0.088 ns
  vs +0.023 ns); T-serial-FAST (−1.280 ns) and T-S4P1-FAST (−0.622 ns) still
  fail at 100 MHz, both slightly worse than the baseline.
- **Time domain, comparisons.** Same Pareto fronts (SLOW: T-serial, T-S4P1,
  T-S8P1; FAST: T-S8P1) and the same D7 evidence for time: the relative gains
  of T-S4P1 and T-S8P1 over T-serial barely move (energy −57.9 % and −80.4 %).
- **Frequency domain.** The trial cuts F-serial-SLOW from 117 to 8 nets and
  from 5.13 to 3.11, still over the amendment, at +31 % area. F-serial-FAST
  does not complete routing. D7 for frequency stays not demonstrated.

## Caveats

- **Sizing record.** The trial runs were made before `run --antenna-trial`
  recorded the applied value, so their `run.json` says `FP_CORE_UTIL` 55. The
  committed manifests take the applied value from `resolved.json` (45) and keep
  the old one as `sizing.FP_CORE_UTIL_in_run_json`.
- **The cell area includes the diodes** and the extra tap/decap cells of the
  larger die; it is not comparable with T44 as a pure logic cost.

## Decisions (T44 owner, @AndresCesana)

1. **Kept as a comparison, not adopted as the T44 baseline.** For time it buys
   the removal of ≤ 3 residual nets per run with +14-18 % area and +5-8 %
   energy, and changes no verdict or ranking. For frequency it is not enough
   (8 nets, 3.11), so it would not remove the need for an antenna rule there.
   T44 keeps the amendment at 55 %, scoped as written in ppa-matrix-18
   (frequency fails it; D7 for frequency is not demonstrated).
2. **F-serial-SLOW: no manual fix.** Hand jumpers on eight nets of one run
   break the same-configuration rule of Measurement Conditions. Under the
   baseline it is recorded as an antenna FAIL in T44.
3. **F-serial-FAST trial: recorded here as FAIL** (global routing congestion
   in antenna repair) and not re-run.
